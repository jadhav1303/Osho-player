📝 Live Captions

Enable your device's built-in Live Captions to read what Osho is saying in real-time.

🍏 iPhone / iPad (iOS 16+)
Settings → Accessibility → Live Captions → Toggle ON
Captions will appear at the top of your screen while audio plays.
🤖 Android
Settings → Accessibility → Live Caption → Toggle ON
Or: press a Volume button, then tap the Live Caption icon below the volume slider.
💻 Chrome Desktop
Chrome Settings → Accessibility → Live Caption → Toggle ON
Or type chrome://settings/accessibility in the address bar.
🪟 Mac
System Settings → Accessibility → Live Captions → Toggle ON
Available on macOS Ventura (13) and later.
🖥️ Windows 11
Settings → Accessibility → Captions → Live Captions → Toggle ON
Or press Win + Ctrl + L to toggle instantly.
